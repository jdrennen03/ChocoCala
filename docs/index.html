How to play
  1. The board has 8 Areas and 2 corrals. The green front row (Areas 1, 3, 5) belongs to the first player (you, or Player 1), the red back row (Areas 8, 7, 6) to the Rival or Player 2, and the yellow Areas 2 and 4 are shared. The green corral on the right is the first player's scoring area; the red one on the left is the other player's.
  2. Each Area starts with three chocobos. Areas 1, 3 and 5 can only be selected by the green player, 6, 7 and 8 only by the red player, and 2 and 4 by both.
  3. Selecting an Area removes all its chocobos and places them one after another, starting from the rear, on the Areas between it and your corral: 1 → 2 → 3 → 4 → 5 → green corral. Tapping an Area previews exactly which chocobo lands where.
  4. If the last chocobo lands in your own corral, you immediately take another turn.
  5. If you overshoot, the extra chocobos continue along the other track: 6 → 4 → 7 → 2 → 8. The opponent's corral is skipped, so you never score for them. The red track is the mirror: 6 → 4 → 7 → 2 → 8 → red corral, overshooting onto 1 → 2 → 3 → 4 → 5.
  6. The game ends when no chocobos are left on the play area, or when a player is due to move and can't. A safety limit also ends it after 100 moves.
  7. End of game: any chocobos still on your own Areas hop into your corral and count for you (1, 3, 5 for the green player; 6, 7, 8 for the red player). Chocobos left on the shared Areas 2 and 4 don't count.
  8. Objective: finish with more jingly in your corral.
  9. Classic mode: every chocobo is worth 1 jingly. Scoring mode: colours have different values, and the second player gets more to make up for moving second:
    • Pink & green (Adenium & Citrullus): 3 first player, 4 second player
    • Black (Korrigan & Pachypodium): 2 first player, 3 second player
    • White (Mandragora): 1 for both
  10. 2 Players: pass the device back and forth. Each player taps their own Areas on their turn.

ChocoCala is based on the rules of Mandragora Mania from Final Fantasy XI, played here with chocobos.
